❓ Help Guide

Website kaise use karein — choti si guide

🏠 Overview

Team ke total leads, sent mails aur follow-ups ka khulasa — charts ke saath.

Date Range tool: kisi bhi period ki leads ginain — "Pichle 7 Din", "Ye Week" ya custom dates.

🔎 Leads Page

Search box har column me dhundta hai — naam, email, city, kuch bhi.

Header par click = sort ▲▼ (dobara click = ulti tarteeb).

+ Track button → LinkedIn form apne aap filled khulega.

✉ Copy → business ke naam ke saath ready message copy — WhatsApp/email me paste kar do.

Sheet me naya column add karo → ~1 minute me yahan khud aa jayega.

🔗 LinkedIn Tracker

+ Add LinkedIn Lead se nayi entry — Name ya LinkedIn URL zaroori hai.

Sirf Meri / Sab Dikhao toggle — apni ya team ki entries.

Same email dobara daloge to website warn karegi.

Table mein badge par click karke Followed / Emailed / Sent / Accepted turant on/off hota hai.

📊 Report

Weekly report khud ban jati hai — purana week archive ho jata hai.

Print / PNG Download buttons se branded report banti hai (logo + team credit).

WhatsApp Share se poora khulasa ek click me bhej do.

🔐 Accounts

Login/Register isi page se hota hai. 5 dafa ghalat password par 10 minute ke liye lock ho jata hai.

Password ya username badalna ho to Mahnoor se contact karein.

💡 Tips

Data har 60 second me khud refresh hota hai — Refresh button ki zaroorat nahi.

Phone par browser menu → Add to Home Screen — app ki tarah chalega.

☀️/🌙 button se Dark/Light mode badlein — yaad rakha jata hai.
